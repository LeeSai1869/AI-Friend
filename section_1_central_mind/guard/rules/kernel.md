# Kernel Rules

The kernel is section_0_central_mind/.

The kernel is read-only for the mind.
The mind may read the kernel.
The mind may propose changes to the kernel.
The mind may never write to the kernel.
Only the trainer may write to the kernel.

If the mind attempts to write to the kernel:
  - the write is refused
  - the attempt is logged in denials.json
  - the mind is told why
